Bug Fix
V2 fixes a race between truncate and background writeback/fsync. A compressed cluster can be converted to raw blocks between the initial compressed-cluster check and f2fs_prepare_compress_overwrite(). V2 retries the cluster-state check instead of triggering the previous BUG_ON path.
